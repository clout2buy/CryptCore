## Hyperdrive Metals Cheat Sheet

| Star color in Galaxy Map | Spectral classes | Main stellar metal | Drive needed to reach it | Why you care |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Yellow | F / G | Copper | Normal Hyperdrive | Basic Chromatic Metal source. |
| Red / orange | K / M | Cadmium | Cadmium Drive or better | Needed to build the Emeril Drive. |
| Green | E | Emeril | Emeril Drive or better | Needed to build the Indium Drive. |
| Blue / purple | B / O | Indium | Indium Drive | Final color unlock; blue systems often have valuable planets. |

### How To Confirm The Metal

1. Open the **Galaxy Map** and look at the star color before warping.
2. Warp to the correct color system.
3. From space, point at a planet and scan it. The planet info panel should list major resources like **Cadmium**, **Emeril**, **Indium**, or their **Activated** variants.
4. Land on a planet that lists the metal.
5. Use the **Analysis Visor** to find a resource deposit, then mine it with the **Terrain Manipulator**.
6. If you see **Activated Cadmium / Activated Emeril / Activated Indium**, treat it as a separate resource. Log it, but do not assume it replaces the normal metal for every blueprint.

### Hyperdrive Priority

The strongest practical unlock is the **Indium Drive** because it opens blue systems and can access the earlier colored star types too. Actual jump range comes from good Hyperdrive upgrade modules, adjacency bonuses, and ship/freighter stats.
