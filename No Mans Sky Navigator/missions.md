## Active Side Goal: Living Ship Hunt

- **Target:** Organic / alien Living Ship like the black, white, and purple ship in the screenshots.
- **Why it matters:** Prestige ship goal. This is a status ship, not just transportation.
- **Start requirement:** Buy a **Void Egg** from the **Quicksilver Synthesis Companion** on the Space Anomaly for **3,200 Quicksilver**.
- **Quest:** Carry the Void Egg, pulse in space until the strange ship contact triggers, then follow **Starbirth**.
- **Important gate:** The later Soul Chamber/portal stage needs glyph access. If glyphs are missing, collect them from Travellers' graves or progress the Artemis/Purge path.
- **Reroll rule:** Save before accepting/claiming the final Living Ship. If the body or colors are bad, reload and reroll before locking it in.
- **After first ship:** Extra Living Ships use another Void Egg and **10,000 Nanites** after Starbirth is complete.

## Next Steps

1. Open the Galaxy Map.
2. Set the route filter to **Atlas Station** or **Current Mission**.
3. Follow the route as far as your ship can jump.
4. If the target is still too far, make smaller jumps along the route.
5. Refuel hyperdrive with Warp Cells or Warp Hypercores.
6. Upgrade hyperdrive range if the route is still annoying.
7. If the Atlas route crosses colored systems, unlock drives in order: **Cadmium Drive -> Emeril Drive -> Indium Drive**.

## Living Ship Checklist

1. Farm or earn **3,200 Quicksilver** from Nexus missions.
2. Buy **Void Egg** from the Space Anomaly.
3. Keep the egg in inventory and pulse in space until contact.
4. Complete each Starbirth organ stage and let parts mature.
5. Make sure portal glyph access is ready before the Soul Chamber stage.
6. Before final claim, make a restore point.
7. Inspect the Living Ship in Photo Mode. Reload if it is ugly.
8. When you get a monster-looking one, claim it and log the system/body/color here.

## Helpful Notes

- You usually do **not** need to jump directly to the final Atlas system.
- The Galaxy Map path may require multiple jumps.
- If the path gets weird, summon the Space Anomaly and talk to Polo/Nada, then reselect the mission.
- New side landmark: **Oishio-Bame** has a big black hole. Do not use it casually if you are trying to stay on the Atlas route.
- Living Ships are organic S-class ships and use biological tech instead of normal starship modules.
- Color-system metals are predictable: red/orange = Cadmium, green = Emeril, blue/purple = Indium.
