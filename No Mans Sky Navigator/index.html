Quick Mission Advice

Open Galaxy Map and set path to Atlas Station or Current Mission.
You don't need to reach the Atlas system in one jump. Chain smaller jumps.
If the route gets confusing, summon the Space Anomaly, talk to Polo/Nada, then reselect the mission.
For a Living Ship: farm 3,200 Quicksilver, buy a Void Egg, pulse until contact, then follow Starbirth.
Before claiming the final Fallen Host, save first. Reload if the color/body is not elite.
Hyperdrive metals: red systems have Cadmium, green systems have Emeril, blue systems have Indium.
